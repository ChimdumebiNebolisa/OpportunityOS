# Verification playbook

- Prefer the opportunity owner’s official page and application page.
- Confirm status, deadline, eligibility, location, funding, and cycle on the authoritative source.
- Preserve unknown values as unknown; do not infer a deadline or eligibility rule from a snippet.
- Treat aggregators, social posts, and search snippets as leads unless policy says otherwise.
- Check for stale pages, closed forms, expired cycles, and annual-cycle differences.
- Record the source URL used for discovery and the official URL used for verification.
